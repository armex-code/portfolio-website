CamFX — your camera as ASCII characters, edge detection or a 3D point cloud.
Click “Start camera” below to begin. Your browser will ask for camera permission.
Drag inside the frame to rotate the point cloud.
Nothing is recorded. Every frame is processed inside your own browser and thrown away immediately — no upload, no server, no saving. Press “Stop camera” or close the page to end it.
