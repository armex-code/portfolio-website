Competitions

Period 2019 — 2024 · Competed in Morocco, USA, Germany, Croatia · Best result 11th worldwide, WRO 2022
Five years of building machines that had to work on one specific day, in front of judges, with no second try. It is where I learned mechanical design, embedded control, and how to get a robot finished when the whole team disagrees about the gearbox.
