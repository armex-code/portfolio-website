Project 01 · Complete

Outdoor LiDAR SLAM at KNU

Context

Autumn 2024, on exchange at Kyungpook National University in South Korea, I joined the Field & Underwater Robotics Lab as an undergraduate intern. The lab works on mobile robots that operate outdoors, where the clean assumptions of indoor SLAM stop holding.

What I built

I tested and iterated on mobile field robots equipped with 2D and 3D LiDAR, implementing and debugging SLAM algorithms in outdoor environments. I ran hardware-in-the-loop testing, handled sensor integration, and set up real-time data capture on Ubuntu.
