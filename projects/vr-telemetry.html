Meta Quest to VEX V5 Teleoperation with Digital Twin

The idea is simple to say and awkward to build: instead of driving a robot from a controller while staring at a screen, put the operator inside the robot's world. I drive a VEX V5 robot from a Meta Quest headset, with a digital twin of the machine updating live next to the real one, so what you see in the headset and what the robot is actually doing stay in step.

I picked the VEX V5 platform because it is the one I know best — five years of competition robotics means I already know how it behaves, which left me free to work on the interesting part, the link between the headset and the robot.

Code is on GitHub: armex-code/quest-v5-teleop
