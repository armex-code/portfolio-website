SLAM & Autonomous Driving

Period 2024 — present · Stack ROS2, C/C++, Python, Linux · Sensing 2D and 3D LiDAR
Field robot SLAM at Kyungpook National University

In autumn 2024 I spent an exchange semester in South Korea and joined the Field & Underwater Robotics Lab at Kyungpook National University as an undergraduate intern. The lab builds robots that work outdoors, which is where the tidy assumptions of indoor SLAM stop being true — no clean walls to match against, changing light, uneven ground, and a machine that vibrates while it drives.

I tested and iterated on mobile field robots fitted with 2D and 3D LiDAR, implementing and debugging SLAM algorithms in outdoor environments. Alongside that I ran hardware-in-the-loop testing, handled sensor integration, and set up real-time data capture on Ubuntu so we could replay a run afterwards instead of guessing what went wrong in the field.
Autonomous vehicle capstone

My final-year capstone continues the same thread: an autonomous vehicle platform, building on the localisation and sensing work above. In progress during the 2026–27 academic year.
